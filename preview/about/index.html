About us.

A small editorial project with a big belief: good decisions beat big hype.

Why Circuit & Shelf exists

Technology can be wonderful. It can also be loud, confusing and expensive. Circuit & Shelf is an independent editorial project focused on practical buying decisions in India, particularly gaming, consumer technology and the experience of owning your gear over time.

We're interested in the questions glossy product launches rarely answer: Will you actually use this feature? How much will the purchase cost after a year? Should you upgrade—or keep something you already love?

How we make content

Our articles combine research, clearly labeled analysis and practical decision frameworks. AI tools may assist with drafting, formatting and early research. AI-generated claims are not hands-on test results, and we will not claim to have tested a product without evidence of that testing.

Editorial material is intended to help readers make their own decisions, not to pressure them into purchases. We aim to correct material inaccuracies when we discover them, and we label changing prices, availability and policies as such.

What you'll find here

  • Gaming console guides that prioritize real game libraries over brand arguments.
  • Ownership and buying checklists designed for everyday Indian consumers.
  • Practical ways to think about cost, accessories, subscriptions and upgrades.
  • Clear disclosures if advertising or affiliate monetization is introduced.

A note on independence

A recommendation should stand on its own reasons. If affiliate links or advertisements appear in the future, they will be disclosed and will not determine our editorial judgment.

Circuit & Shelf launched as a publication project in October 2026. We're building carefully, one useful story at a time.
