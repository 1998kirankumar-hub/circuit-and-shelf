Independence and product recommendations

Our buying advice is based on practical criteria such as use case, availability, compatibility and total cost of ownership. We do not offer a guarantee that any product is right for every buyer. Please independently verify specifications, current pricing and seller terms.

AI-assisted drafting

AI-assisted tools may be used to structure research and draft content. We aim to distinguish general advice from claims based on first-hand testing. We do not publish fabricated benchmarks, imaginary interviews or invented ownership anecdotes as real evidence.

Affiliate links and sponsored work

This initial launch does not contain paid affiliate links or sponsored placements. If those are introduced, applicable articles will clearly identify the material connection. Readers should not assume that a future link is sponsored unless it is labeled.

Automatically refreshed linked headlines

The newsroom section links to publisher RSS headlines that refresh automatically. These links are clearly attributed, are not our own reporting, and do not imply that we independently verified each claim in a news headline. For full reporting, readers should open the original publication.

Advertising

Advertising is not active on the initial website. If we become eligible for Google AdSense or another network, advertising placements and our privacy policy will be updated to meet applicable rules. The presence of advertising will not guarantee a favourable editorial view.

Corrections

Technology products, subscriptions and store policies change. When we identify a material error, we intend to correct the article and update its editorial date as appropriate. We will establish a public corrections email alongside our site's dedicated domain.
