Privacy policy.

A clear explanation of the limited data this site may handle.

Effective date

26 October 2026. This policy applies to Circuit & Shelf's published website. The policy will be updated if our site features or data handling change.

Information and cookies

In this initial website build, we do not operate visitor account registration, comment databases or a custom contact submission system. Our site's theme preference is stored locally in your browser using localStorage so the appearance you choose can be remembered. We do not receive your theme preference on our server.

Our hosting provider may process technical request logs, such as IP address, device details and timestamps, to serve the website and maintain its security. The applicable hosting provider policy will govern that processing.

Advertising and analytics

Advertising and Google Analytics are not enabled in this initial release. If Google AdSense or other ad partners are added, this policy will be revised to explain the relevant cookies, personalized advertising choices and consent mechanisms required for visitors.

Google explains how information is used when its partner sites or apps use its services at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Automated headlines and third-party requests

The homepage may request a publicly accessible headlines JSON file from GitHub's raw content service to show linked publisher headlines. GitHub may process standard technical request information under its privacy policy. No visitors need to sign into GitHub to read headlines.

External links

Articles may link to independent manufacturers, stores or services. We do not control their privacy practices; please review their policies before using their websites.

Your choices and contact

You can clear locally stored theme preferences through your browser settings. We plan to add a dedicated editorial contact address after the publication's domain and inbox are confirmed. Until then, this site does not collect enquiries through a web form.

Changes to this policy

We will update the effective date and text if our practices change. Please revisit this page after we introduce ads, analytics or additional functionality.
